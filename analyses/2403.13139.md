# Generating Automatic Feedback on UI Mockups with Large Language Models

*Analysis for your experiment-brief proposal · RQ2 Communicating · based on the full text*

## What the paper did
Duan et al. built a Figma plugin that converts a mockup's layer tree into JSON and asks GPT-4 to check it against design guidelines, then rephrases each violation as constructive advice linked to the element. Three designers rated output on 51 UIs across three guideline sets; 12 expert designers' heuristic evaluations were compared with GPT-4's on 12 UIs; and 12 designers used the plugin over 2 to 3 revision rounds.

## Key findings
- Across 51 UIs, 52% of suggestions were rated accurate (19% partially) and 49% helpful or very helpful; rater agreement was slight (kappa about 0.10).
- Against experts, GPT-4 had lower precision (0.603 vs. 0.829) but similar recall (0.380 vs. 0.336) and F1 (0.466 vs. 0.478); humans found 62 violations GPT-4 missed, GPT-4 found 9 humans missed.
- Utility dropped with iteration: about 52% accurate and 47% helpful in round one, 39% and 33% later.
- All 12 designers valued catching subtle errors and text fixes; all 12 also saw guidelines over-applied without context, and 8 noted missed visual issues because the model read JSON, not the rendered screen.
- 9 of 12 would use it; 8 of 12 did not see errors as dangerous because a human reviews output, though some worried about novice overtrust.

## Why it relates to your idea
Your system has a model read a design artifact (two code-prototype branches) and output judgments a designer acts on: what changed, what to measure, what could go wrong. This paper shows what designers accept from such a tool and where model judgments on structure (not pixels) fail.

## What you can take from it
- Link every AI statement to the element it is about, as the plugin does (click to focus, hover highlight): anchor the hypothesis and each risk card to the highlighted change in the side-by-side prototypes.
- Expect structural blind spots: their model missed overlap and alignment from JSON alone, so your diff-based inference may miss changes that matter visually but not in code; consider including screenshots in the model input.
- Plan for repetition and over-application: dedupe risk cards and cap them at 3 to 5, as your proposal does, since repeated or generic feedback lowered helpfulness.
- Their precision/recall comparison against experts is a template for your analyst blind review of AI-generated hypotheses and risk cards.

## Limits for your use
- Static single screens and heuristic evaluation, not experiments or metrics.
- Expert designers with small samples (12) and low inter-rater agreement.
