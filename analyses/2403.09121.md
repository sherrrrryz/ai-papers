# OutlineSpark: Igniting AI-powered Presentation Slides Creation from Computational Notebooks through Outlines

*Analysis for your experiment-brief proposal · RQ3 Reading results · based on the full text*

## What the paper did
The authors built OutlineSpark, a JupyterLab extension that helps people turn an analysis notebook into presentation slides by writing an outline first. It has three linked panels: a notebook overview that shows cells as keyword cards with a bar marking relevance to the selected outline item, an outline panel with LLM topic suggestions, and an editable slides panel. A GPT-3.5 backend maps up to 5 cells to each outline item and writes one bullet per cell. The evaluation had N = 12 postgraduate researchers building a 5 to 10 slide deck from a 42-cell house-price notebook, with no baseline condition.

## Key findings
- SUS was 85.2.
- The outline-centered design got a median rating of 6.5 out of 7, and 11 of 12 participants praised it. Ten of 12 started with an outline.
- Topic recommendation was the weakest feature (median 4 out of 7). Slide customization was only weakly positive (5.5 out of 7).
- Outline-based work took 36.1% of task time and cell-selection refinement 18.6%.
- Users wanted control over generation. One said, "I don't trust LLM is capable of generating high quality slides for my entire outline at one time."

## Why it relates to your idea
Your results phase has a similar step: turning analysis output into a story a non-analyst can act on (conclusion, funnel on the prototype, next hypotheses). OutlineSpark shows a way to do this with AI while keeping each statement traceable to its source cell. Your division of labour (Pandas computes, templates draw, the model only writes captions) is similar in spirit.

## What you can take from it
- Link every AI-written caption in the results view to the number or chart it describes, as OutlineSpark links bullets to cells. This lets a reader check a sentence against its evidence.
- Keep the narrative structure under human control. Users preferred writing the outline themselves and rated AI topic suggestions lowest, which supports keeping AI-suggested next hypotheses as optional suggestions.
- Generate small pieces one at a time rather than the whole story at once, matching the participant's distrust of whole-outline generation.
- The gap it leaves: OutlineSpark helps analysts communicate. Your project is about the receiving end, where the reader cannot check the notebook.

## Limits for your use
- Users were postgraduate researchers who could read the notebook, not designers or PMs.
- Small N (12), a single notebook, no baseline, and it measures usability rather than whether the audience understood the result.
